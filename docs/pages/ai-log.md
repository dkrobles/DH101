# AI Use Log

---

**Date:** Week 1 – This Person Does Not Exist  
**Tool Used:** ThisPersonDoesNotExist / AI-generated human face  
**Task / Prompt:** Analyze and document how AI creates realistic human faces that do not belong to real people.  
**What the AI Suggested:** Generated random synthetic human portraits using machine learning trained on datasets of real faces.  
**What I Accepted, Changed, or Rejected:** Accepted the generated face as the artifact and focused my own writing on data sources, realism, missing identity, and ethical concerns.  
**Why:** I used the AI output mainly as evidence for analysis rather than creative direction, emphasizing how AI constructs realism without human identity.

**Date:** Week 3 – AI Selfies  
**Tool Used:** DALL·E / ChatGPT image generation  
**Task / Prompt:** Transform selfies into alternate visual versions (sunny idealized self and surreal purple-eyed creative self).

**Why:** I wanted the images to contrast public appearance with inner creativity.
