# How I Use AI

Describe how you approach AI use in this course.

## Tools Used
- ChatGPT (brainstorming, outlining, editing, dialogue refinement)

## What I Asked AI To Do
- Generate game ideas about human–AI relationships
- Suggest possible branching narrative structures
- Help organize interview-style dialogue
- Refine wording and improve clarity in early drafts

## Concrete Examples of AI Output
1. ChatGPT suggested a narrative branch where the interviewer begins trusting the AI more over time. I kept the general idea but rewrote the dialogue to sound more natural and less robotic.

2. AI generated several possible endings for the project, including one where the AI completely manipulates the player. I rejected that version because it felt too cliché and did not match the more emotionally complex tone I wanted.

## What I Kept
- The overall structure of the interview concept
- Some early branching ideas
- A few dialogue prompts that inspired later revisions

## What I Changed
- Rewrote most dialogue in my own voice
- Simplified the branching structure to make choices clearer
- Adjusted character responses to better reflect my themes about trust, emotion, and technology

## What I Discarded
- Dialogue that felt unrealistic or repetitive
- Overly complex storylines
- Generic “AI takeover” scenarios that did not fit the project’s tone

## Verification and Revision
I reviewed all AI-generated suggestions critically instead of accepting them automatically. Some outputs included repetitive phrasing or unrealistic emotional reactions, so I revised them to better fit the project and maintain a more believable conversation style.

## Ethics and Reflection
This project connects to course discussions about the limits and biases of generative AI. Readings by Bender, D’Ignazio, Klein, and Crawford helped me think about AI as a tool that can reproduce patterns and stereotypes from existing data rather than create truly original ideas. Because of this, I treated AI output as a starting point for brainstorming instead of a replacement for human creativity or judgment. I made the final decisions about story structure, tone, and meaning throughout the project.

## My AI Use Policy
I used AI as a support tool, not as a replacement for my own creativity. All final creative decisions, revisions, and interpretations were made by me.
